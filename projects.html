04

Bat-wing

Impact-Transforming Saddle

Laboratorio di Progettazione 3 · Bachelor's Thesis

Problem: E-bike and electric micromobility users face three critical issues: micro-impacts from rough roads cause discomfort and instability, limited battery range restricts travel, and poor seating posture leads to ischial inflammation for 85% of casual cyclists.

Solution: An adaptive saddle with a split "wing" design that automatically adjusts to different rider positions. Integrated shock absorbers dampen micro-impacts, while a kinetic energy recovery system transforms oscillation energy into electricity to extend battery life. The design combines ergonomic research with innovative materials for optimal comfort.

Result: 70% reduction in transmitted vibrations, 10% battery range extension through energy recovery, automatic adaptation to various riding positions. Developed under the guidance of Prof. Alessandra Rinaldi with tutors Jonathan Lagrimino and Sara Viviani.

Year 2024
Role Concept, 3D Modeling, Prototyping, Research
Tools Fusion 360, Rhinoceros, 3D Printing
Target E-bike & micromobility users
