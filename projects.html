SHOCK!

Energy-Harvesting Wheel — Bachelor's thesis

E-bike wheel with a 3D-printed TPU lattice tire that absorbs impacts, integrated piezoelectric generators that convert stress into electricity, and a battery in the hub. No punctures, more comfort, more sustainability.

Year 2024
Role Design, Prototyping
Tools Fusion 360, 3D Printing

Solar-Foldable Scooter

Universal Epilator

04

Bat-wing

Impact-Transforming Saddle

Laboratorio di Progettazione 3 · Bachelor's Thesis

Problem: E-bike users face micro-impacts, limited range, and poor posture.

Solution: Adaptive saddle with split wing design, integrated shock absorbers, and kinetic energy recovery.

Result: 70% vibration reduction, 10% battery extension, automatic position adaptation.

Year 2024
Role Concept, 3D Modeling, Prototyping, Research
Tools Fusion 360, Rhinoceros, 3D Printing
Target E-bike & micromobility users
